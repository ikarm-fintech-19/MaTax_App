# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Enterprise managers (EURL, SARL, SPA), registered merchants (RC), individual professionals, and accountants or tax experts in Algeria. They operate in a complex, shifting, and intimidating regulatory landscape (Loi de Finances 2026). They need to calculate obligations and file tax documents (G50, IBS, IRG) without calculation errors or late-filing penalties.

## Product Purpose

MaTax serves as an intelligent compliance assistant that automates calculations, populates official DGI declarations (monthly/quarterly G50, annual G11, G04), and lists upcoming deadlines on a personalized dashboard. Success means stress-free, 100% compliant filings, hours saved in manual calculations, and seamless collaboration between businesses and accountants.

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone**: Reassuring, expert, clear, and professional.
- **3-Word Personality**: Confident, Precise, Compliant.
- **Emotional Goals**: Reassurance, certainty, and peace of mind when facing tax audits or deadlines.

## Anti-references

- **Clunky Government Portals**: Avoid confusing forms with endless unvalidated text inputs (like Jibayatic/DGI portal) that offer no immediate validation or guidance.
- **SaaS Dashboards Clichés**: Avoid glowing dark-mode-only developer dashboards, neon accents, floating cards, or high-chroma gradients.
- **Jargon-Heavy Sheets**: Avoid presenting a wall of unexplained tax categories without direct tooltips or references.

## Design Principles

1. **Legally Grounded Authority**: Every calculation or form field must link directly to or cite official codes (e.g., _Article 104 of LF 2026_).
2. **Bilingual Symmetry**: Design forms and navigation to support LTR (French) and RTL (Arabic) layouts naturally, without visual degradation or broken boundaries.
3. **Task-Specific Focus**: Keep screens highly functional. Use flat tonal layering instead of shadows and floating cards. Use progressive disclosure to reveal complex inputs only when needed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA Compliance**: High-contrast text on all neutral backgrounds.
- **Color Blindness Accommodations**: Avoid using red or green as the sole indicators of error or success; always pair them with clear helper icons and descriptive text.
- **Responsive Layouts**: Accessible touch targets (minimum 44px) and adaptive grid columns for mobile and tablet users.
